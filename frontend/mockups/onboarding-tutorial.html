Welcome to Mapcess

Spot accessibility issues in your city, report them, and help us route people around obstacles.

What you'll learn A quick tour of the four most common things people report — ramps, sidewalks, elevators, and doors — and how to file your first report in under a minute.

Ramps

  • Slope ≤ 10% — steeper blocks wheelchairs accessible
  • Clear width ≥ 100 cm — room for a walker + helper accessible
  • Handrails on both sides at 90 cm (extra rail at 70 cm for shorter users) accessible elderly visibility
  • Tactile warning strip at the top and bottom of the ramp visibility
slope = H ÷ L · accessible when ≤ 10%
Tip Capture the full slope from the side so others can judge how steep it is.
directions_walk

Sidewalks

  • Clear width ≥ 150 cm — wheelchairs need to pass each other accessible
  • Overhead clearance ≥ 220 cm — no low branches or signs accessible visibility elderly
  • Contrasting strip along the curb edge so it's visible against the road visibility
Top-down view of the sidewalk
Tip Photograph blockages or surface damage from a pedestrian's perspective so reviewers can see the obstacle the way you did.
elevator

Elevators

  • Door clear width ≥ 90 cm when fully open accessible
  • Braille labels on every button and voice announcement visibility
  • Visual floor display + flashing emergency alarm hearing
Top-down view of the cabin
Tip Note when it's out of service or has narrow doors — include the building entrance for context.
door_front

Doors

  • Clear width ≥ 90 cm when fully open accessible
  • Lever handle (not a knob) — easier with weak grip accessible elderly
  • Glass panels marked with contrasting bands at eye level visibility
Front view — entrance door
Tip Show the handle and any threshold step so reviewers can see the obstacle clearly.
